# Open Source Roadmap

This roadmap focuses on genuine community value and contributor accessibility.

## Goal

Make Targilim easy for external contributors to understand, test, improve, and review without weakening source-backed math requirements or repository verification gates.

## Immediate priorities

1. Contributor onboarding: clear contribution guide, issue template, and PR template.
2. Small verifiable tasks: accessibility, RTL/BiDi robustness, documentation, tests, and tooling.
3. Public evidence: keep issues narrowly scoped and record verification in pull requests.
4. Community health: welcome external contributors, review promptly, and preserve attribution.

## What not to do

- Do not create fake contributors, synthetic pull requests, or meaningless issues.
- Do not split one trivial change into many PRs merely to inflate counts.
- Do not add unverified mathematical content.
- Do not expose student data, credentials, or private materials.

## Contribution targets

The repository should aim for sustained participation by independent contributors rather than raw activity volume. Track unique external contributors with merged pull requests over a rolling 12-month window and maintain a backlog of genuine `good first issue` tasks.
